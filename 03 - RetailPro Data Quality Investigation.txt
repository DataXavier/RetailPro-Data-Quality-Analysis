1. Purpose of Investigation

The purpose of this investigation was to examine the RetailPro inventory dataset and identify potential data quality problems before making any corrections.

The investigation focused on duplicate records, missing information, inconsistent formatting, and unusual inventory quantities.

2. Findings

Finding 1: Potential Duplicate Records

Product IDs 101 and 103 appear to be potential duplicate records.

Both records contain:

- Product Name: Laptop
- Price: $899.99
- Stock Quantity: 25
- Category: Electronics / electronics

The records have different product IDs, but their product information matches except for category capitalization.

Recommendation: Investigate both records to determine whether they represent the same product before removing or combining anything.

Finding 2: Missing Information

Product ID 104 (Desk) has a missing price.

The price field contains NULL, indicating that the information is missing or unknown.

Recommendation: Verify the correct price with an approved business source before using the record in inventory valuation calculations.

Finding 3: Inconsistent Category Formatting

Product IDs 105 and 106 have inconsistent category formatting.

- Product 105: Accessories
- Product 106: accessories

Both records appear to belong to the same category, but the capitalization differs.

Recommendation: Standardize category names using an approved naming convention.

Finding 4: Zero Inventory Quantity

Product ID 105 (Keyboard) has a stock quantity of zero.

This should not automatically be considered a data error because the product may genuinely be out of stock.

Recommendation: Verify the quantity against the inventory system or warehouse records before making any changes.

4. Conclusion

The investigation identified several potential data quality issues within the RetailPro inventory dataset.

These include potential duplicate product records, missing pricing information, inconsistent category formatting, and inventory quantities requiring verification.

Before making corrections, each issue should be investigated using approved business rules and reliable source information.

The next step is to use Google Sheets to examine the dataset, document the identified issues, and prepare a separate cleaned version of the inventory records.
