RetailPro Inc.

Process Improvement Recommendations

1. Objective

Recommend improvements to RetailPro's inventory data management process to reduce data quality issues and improve the accuracy of inventory reporting.

2. Issues Identified

The inventory dataset contained:

- Inconsistent category capitalization.
- A missing price for product ID 104 (Desk).
- Two Laptop records that may be duplicates.
- A Keyboard record with zero stock quantity that requires verification.

3. Recommended Improvements

Issue| Recommendation| Expected Benefit
Inconsistent category formatting| Use a standardized category list or dropdown in the inventory system.| Consistent reporting by category.
Missing product prices| Require a valid price before a product record can be approved.| Fewer incomplete records.
Potential duplicate products| Add duplicate checks using product ID and product details. Require review before merging or deleting records.| Reduce duplicate records without losing valid data.
Zero stock quantity| Confirm zero quantities against warehouse inventory records.| More reliable stock reporting.
Recurring data quality problems| Perform regular data quality checks and maintain a cleaning log.| Identify problems earlier.

4. Recommended Process

1. Warehouse or purchasing staff enter inventory information.
2. The system checks required fields and approved category values.
3. Potential duplicates and missing values are flagged for review.
4. Inventory staff verify flagged records against the source system.
5. Approved corrections are made and documented.
6. The data analyst runs validation checks.
7. Management receives an updated inventory report.

5. Success Measures

- All product records have a unique product ID.
- Required product fields are complete.
- Category names follow the approved standard.
- Potential duplicates are reviewed and documented.
- Inventory quantities are verified against warehouse records.
- Inventory reports pass validation checks before distribution.

6. Conclusion

Implementing standardized data entry, validation checks, duplicate review, and regular monitoring can improve RetailPro's inventory data quality. These changes can help management make more reliable inventory and purchasing decisions.
